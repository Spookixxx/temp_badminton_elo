Who are we?

Dropshot is a community badminton society, where people can come and play the best sport (situational). The management team takes no profit from our sessions - every penny made goes back into the group. So if you're interested, drop us an email!

Dropshot is made for all level of players, but mainly focused on beginners and intermediate players wanting to play against people of their level.
